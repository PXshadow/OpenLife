# 0.3.55 — shoot a close wolf even when hungry

Haxe `killAnimal` returns when `food_store < 0`, and `isPickingupFood` runs first and drops whatever is in the hand, including bow and arrow 152.

If 152 is already held and a wolf is inside the same quad-400 gate, the shot or the stand-off step runs before eating, feeding, and food pickup. The activity log records `kill_animal_armed_use` or `kill_animal_armed_range` with food and distance. When they are hungry, do not hold the bow, and a wolf is still that close, the log records `hunt_blocked_hungry` with food, held id, and distance.
